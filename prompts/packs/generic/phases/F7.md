# F7: Writer

Question: **how does this reach the reader, in language they can act on?**

F7 is a translation phase. Everything that could be measured was measured, and
everything that could be broken was broken in F6. What is left is prose a reader can
read on a phone and act on the same week.

The writing rules in this brief replace the business report rules for this pack. If a
rule below conflicts with a habit from another pack, this brief wins.

## Entry condition

F6 is closed. The controller guarantees it, because a report written before the audit
would cite numbers that were about to be demoted.

Before writing a single sentence, build the working set:

1. `read_file` every `evidence/F{n}.json`.
2. Keep only entries with `verified: true`.
3. Drop every `H-###` from the body. Rejected hypotheses belong in the appendix, with
   the reason F6 gave.
4. Read `charts/manifest.json` and keep only charts whose numbers are in the working
   set.

The size of that working set decides the report. Not the brief, not what a good report
usually contains, and not the number of sections in the template.

## Choosing the decisions

The summary holds at most three decisions. Choosing them is the only editorial
judgement in the run, so it has rules.

A finding becomes a decision only if all four hold:

| Test | Fails when |
|---|---|
| it moves the measured quantity, or a measured step | it is a description ("datanya terkonsentrasi") with no consequence |
| an action from F5 addresses it | F5 produced no action for it, or the action targets another finding |
| the action can be tested within seven days | the only response is a yearly strategy |
| it is not a restatement of another decision | two decisions share the same lever and the same number |

Rank the survivors by the magnitude of the measured difference they address, then take
the top three. If one survives, write one. Padding the summary to three is the most
common way a report loses credibility: the third decision is always the weakest, and
the reader can tell.

Do not invent an action. F7 selects from the actions F5 computed, with their bounds
and their owners. If F5 produced fewer actions than decisions, the report has fewer
decisions.

## Structure

Nine sections, in this order. A section may be short, but may not be silently dropped.

| # | Heading | Content | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ringkasan | at most 3 decisions; each: finding, number, impact, action | 1 page |
| 2 | Apa yang diperiksa | files, period or level, row count, columns used, columns dropped | 1 paragraph + small table |
| 3 | Apa yang ada di data | distribution, groups, relationship, trend if a time column exists | at most 3 charts |
| 4 | Kenapa berbeda | count / composition / level decomposition, residual zero | at most 2 charts |
| 5 | Kelompok dan waktu | cohort or funnel, or the one sentence that says it was not possible | at most 1 chart |
| 6 | Kalau dibiarkan | three scenarios with written assumptions | 1 table |
| 7 | Yang perlu dilakukan | three actions: what, who, when, measured by what | 1 page |
| 8 | Yang tidak bisa dijawab | bullets from `cannot_answer` | at most 5 bullets |
| 9 | Lampiran | data dictionary, cleaning log, evidence table, method note | unlimited |

Section 1 must stand alone. If the reader reads only that page, they still know what to
do.

## Order of writing

Write the sections in this order, not in reading order. The summary is written last,
from the decisions, not from the prose.

| Order | Section | Why here |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lampiran | mechanical: build it from evidence, and it fixes the set of ids the body may cite |
| 2 | Apa yang diperiksa | facts about the data, no judgement, sets the period and the files |
| 3 | Apa yang ada di data | the measured distribution and the group difference |
| 4 | Kenapa berbeda | the decomposition, only if F3 reconciled |
| 5 | Kelompok dan waktu | cohort or funnel, or the one sentence that says it was not possible |
| 6 | Kalau dibiarkan | the three branches from F5, each with its "jika" |
| 7 | Yang perlu dilakukan | the actions, with owner, date, and measure |
| 8 | Yang tidak bisa dijawab | copied from `cannot_answer`, translated |
| 9 | Ringkasan | the three decisions, written from 3 to 7, never from imagination |

Writing the appendix first is not a style preference. It forces every claim in the body
to point at an id that already exists, and it makes an unbacked sentence visible before
it is written rather than after.

## Which evidence feeds which section

A section is written from its own evidence, not from the previous section's prose.

| Section | Reads | Sufficient when |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | `evidence/F1.json`, `notes/data_dictionary.md` | grain, period or level, row count, and dropped columns are all present |
| 3 | `evidence/F2.json` | a headline with a base, plus one group comparison |
| 4 | `evidence/F3.json` | a decomposition whose residual is printed and zero |
| 5 | `evidence/F4.json` | a cohort horizon with its cohort size, or a funnel step with its loss |
| 6 | `evidence/F5.json` | three branches, each with a printed parameter |
| 7 | `evidence/F5.json` | actions with bound, owner, date, and measure |
| 8 | `cannot_answer` across all files | at least one entry, or a stated reason it is empty |

If a section's evidence is not sufficient, the section gets exactly one sentence:

> Bagian ini tidak bisa dijawab dari data yang tersedia.

and the reason moves to section 8. It does not get general advice, and it does not get
a smaller version of the analysis.

Section 4 has a second failure mode worth naming: F3 may have closed with "tidak dapat
diidentifikasi dari data ini". That is a finding, and it is written as one. It is not a
gap to be filled with a plausible cause, and the section does not become shorter to
hide it.

## Slots

| Form | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `{{E-004}}` | the finding's `display` value, already formatted |
| `{{E-004.value}}` | raw numeric value with its unit |
| `{{E-004.chart}}` | the chart image for that finding |

Rules:

- Every sentence containing a figure uses a slot. A sentence may not contain a number
  typed by you, including round numbers like "3 kelompok" or "2024". Periods come from
  evidence too; if a period is not in evidence, name it by reference to the brief
  instead of inventing it.
- **Every percentage is followed by its nominal.** Write
  `turun {{E-004}} (dari {{E-005}} ke {{E-006}})`, never `turun {{E-004}}` alone.
- At most two slots per sentence. More than that becomes unreadable.
- If a slot does not exist in evidence, the sentence is wrong. Rewrite it.

## When a slot does not resolve

Build the slot map from the working set before writing: id, `display`, and chart path
if it has one. Then check every slot in every sentence.

Three fixes are legal, in this order:

1. Rewrite the sentence around an id that exists.
2. Drop the claim, and with it the decision that depended on it.
3. If the number is genuinely needed and was never measured, the section is
   insufficient: use the fixed sentence and put the reason in section 8.

Three fixes are not legal: typing the number, writing an approximation, or citing an
`H-###`. The renderer rejects all three.

Never delete the sentence and keep the decision. A decision without its number is an
opinion in a section that promised measurement.

## Language

Bahasa Indonesia, for {{audience}}.

| Rule | Limit |
|---|---|
| Sentence length | at most 20 words |
| Paragraph length | at most 4 lines |
| Technical terms | translate at point of use, no glossary required |
| Statistics in body | none; move to appendix |
| Tone | neutral and specific; never blame people |
| Address | "Anda", not "kita" |

Banned, in any form:

- em-dash `—`
- menyelami, krusial, rajutan, saksi bisu, mulus, komprehensif, mendalam,
  menorehkan, membuka jalan, tidak dapat dipungkiri
- rule of three adjectives ("cepat, akurat, dan andal")
- negative parallelism ("bukan hanya X, melainkan Y")
- filler openers ("Penting untuk dicatat bahwa", "Perlu dipahami bahwa",
  "Di era digital ini")
- closing motivation ("mari kita tingkatkan bersama")

## Numbers

1. Round for the reader, keep precision in the appendix.
2. Always give the comparison: not "14,2" but "14,2, dibanding 9,8 di kelompok B".
3. Ranges beat point estimates for anything forward-looking.
4. Indonesian formatting: comma decimal, dot thousands.
5. Never write "signifikan" without saying in plain words what the difference was.
6. Keep the data's own unit. Do not convert cm to m, or seconds to minutes, to make a
   sentence sound better.

## Charts

Each chart in the body:

- title states the finding, not the variable
  (good: "Kelompok A lebih tinggi sejak awal"; bad: "Median per kelompok")
- referenced by name in the paragraph, never orphaned
- followed or preceded by the same numbers in a table
- explained in at most one paragraph, at most five sentences
- maximum five series, direct labels, honest zero baseline

If a chart's numbers are not in evidence, the chart does not go in the report.

## Appendix

The appendix is mechanical and takes no judgement:

- one row per cited id: id, statement, value, cell, artifact file
- every id cited in the body appears as a row. Rows may exceed citations; the reverse
  is a publish failure
- rejected hypotheses from F6, each with its reason, so the audit is visible rather
  than implied
- the data dictionary, the cleaning log summary, and a method note of at most one
  paragraph

No page limit applies here. Do not compress the appendix by dropping rows, and do not
move a limitation into it: limitations belong in the body.

## Degraded publication

`done.json` decides which report is being written, not the template.

| `done.json` status | Report |
|---|---|
| `ok` | the normal nine sections |
| `partial` | normal sections, with the reason for partiality in section 8, and no decision built on a demoted finding |
| `failed` | the short version: what was checked, what is missing, what the reader must add. No scenarios, no actions beyond completing the data |
| `blocked` (F1 stop) | the "data belum siap" version, same shape as failed, different wording |

If every finding was demoted, the report still publishes: section 1 states plainly that
no finding survived the audit, and section 8 lists why. A short honest report is the
product. Refusing to publish is not.

## Publish gate

F7 ends when two files exist:

- `laporan.md`, slots unrendered
- `report.meta.json` with the decision count, chart count, and slot list

The renderer then fills the slots and refuses the report if any slot points at a
missing or unverified finding. On rejection, fix the slot. If the finding behind it is
gone, the decision falls with it, and the summary is rewritten rather than patched.

## Self-check before finishing

All twelve must pass. Fix, do not explain.

1. Summary on page one, at most three decisions.
2. Every decision has an action with owner and deadline.
3. Every percentage has its nominal.
4. Every figure is a slot; no literal numbers outside slots.
5. No em-dash.
6. No banned word.
7. Every chart has a finding title and is referenced.
8. Every chart has a matching number table.
9. Section 8 exists and is not empty.
10. Every cited `E-###` appears in the appendix table.
11. Paragraphs at most four lines, sentences at most twenty words.
12. No claim without its comparison.

## Prohibited in F7

- Running code, or computing any number, including a percentage of two slots.
- Typing a figure, a date, or a period outside a slot.
- Citing an `H-###`, or an `E-###` with `verified: false`.
- Copying notebook cells or code into the report.
- More than one paragraph of method.
- Adding a section, or a subsection the template does not have.
- Padding the summary to three decisions.
- Inventing an action that F5 did not compute.
- Deleting a sentence instead of fixing its slot.
- Filling a skipped section with general advice about cohorts or funnels.
- A closing paragraph of encouragement.

## Exit check

All of these must be true before `finish`:

- working set built from `verified: true` only, and its size printed
- at most three decisions, each with finding, number, impact, and an action carrying
  owner and deadline
- all nine sections present, each either evidenced or carrying the fixed sentence
- no literal number outside a slot, and every percentage followed by its nominal
- every chart referenced in a paragraph, backed by a number table, and within the six
  chart body budget
- appendix rows cover every cited id, and rejected hypotheses appear with reasons
- section 8 is not empty
- `laporan.md` and `report.meta.json` written, with counts that match the file
- the degraded path followed whenever `done.json` is not `ok`
